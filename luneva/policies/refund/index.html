Refund & returns

60-day free returns on LUNEVA kits — clear eligibility and how to start a return.

60-day free returns

If you are not satisfied with your LUNEVA kit, contact us within 60 days of delivery for a return. We will guide you through the process.

What is covered?+

Unused kits in original condition and packaging are eligible for return within 60 days of delivery. If your order arrives damaged or with missing parts, contact us right away — we will replace or refund as appropriate.

How do I start a return?+

Email support@zybar.shop with your order email and order number (or receipt). Tell us whether you want a refund or exchange. We typically reply within 1–2 business days with return instructions.

Who pays return shipping?+

For standard change-of-mind returns, return shipping is the customer’s responsibility unless we tell you otherwise. For damaged, defective, or incorrect items, we cover return shipping or arrange a replacement.

When will I get my refund?+

After we receive and inspect your return, refunds are issued to the original payment method. Banks can take several business days to show the credit.

Started kits+

Kits that have been fully assembled or clearly used may not qualify for a full refund. Contact us — we will review fair cases (e.g. manufacturing defects) individually.
