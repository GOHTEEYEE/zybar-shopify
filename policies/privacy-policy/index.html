Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

01

Information Collection

We collect minimal data necessary for order fulfillment, including your name, shipping address, and email (for order tracking and support).

02

Cookies

Our site uses cookies and similar technologies to operate the store, keep items in your cart, measure site traffic, and improve your browsing experience.

03

Analytics and Advertising

We use our own analytics tools and the Meta Pixel (Facebook / Instagram) to understand how visitors use the site, which pages are viewed, and whether ads lead to purchases. Meta may receive browser and device information, pages visited, and conversion events such as add to cart and purchase.

You can control ad tracking through your browser settings, ad blockers, or Meta’s ad preferences.

04

Third-Party Disclosure

We never sell your data to third parties. We only share information with partners essential for completing your order (for example, shipping carriers and payment processors).
