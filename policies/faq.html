Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ZYBAR. Still need help? Contact Support.

1. How long does shipping take?

Every ZYBAR piece is carefully handcrafted before shipping.

  • Standard Shipping: 14–18 business days
  • Priority Shipping: 7–14 business days

Once your order ships, you’ll receive a tracking number by email so you can follow its journey every step of the way.

2. Where are ZYBAR products made?

Every ZYBAR piece is carefully handcrafted by our team and shipped worldwide from Malaysia.

We focus on premium craftsmanship, meticulous quality control, and attention to detail to ensure every piece arrives ready to impress.

3. Does it come ready to hang?

Yes.

Every artwork arrives ready to display with all the necessary mounting hardware included.

Installation only takes a few minutes, allowing you to transform your space effortlessly.

4. How is it powered?

You can choose between two power options:

USB Powered

Connect directly to any USB power source. Perfect for desks, bedrooms, gaming setups, or offices.

Dual Power (USB + Battery)

Works with both USB power and batteries for greater flexibility. Ideal if you prefer a cleaner, cordless display.

5. Can I use it in my country?

Absolutely.

ZYBAR ships worldwide, and every piece is compatible with standard USB power sources used internationally.

No matter where you live, you can enjoy your artwork right out of the box.

6. Is it bright enough?

Yes — our lighting is designed to create atmosphere, not overwhelm your space.

Instead of flooding the room with harsh light, ZYBAR uses selective illumination to highlight the car’s iconic silhouette, creating a premium, cinematic look whether it’s displayed in your garage, office, bedroom, or gaming room.

7. Can I customize my own car?

Yes.

Our Custom Collection allows you to transform your own vehicle into a handcrafted illuminated artwork.

Simply upload a photo of your car, choose your preferences, and our design team will create a unique piece made just for you.

Start your Custom Made order →

8. What if my order arrives damaged?

We carefully inspect every piece before shipping.

If your order arrives damaged during transit, simply contact us within 48 hours of delivery and include a few photos of the damage.

We’ll work with you as quickly as possible to provide a replacement or the appropriate solution.

9. What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-Day Easy Returns policy for eligible products.

Items must be returned in their original condition and packaging.

Please note that personalized or custom-made products cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.

For full details, please visit our Refund Policy.

10. Why choose ZYBAR?

ZYBAR isn’t just wall decor.

It’s designed for people who love cars and want their passion to become part of their living space.

Every piece combines premium materials, carefully controlled lighting, and handcrafted detail to create an artwork that feels timeless—not just another LED decoration.

Our goal isn’t simply to light up a wall. It’s to create a space that feels unmistakably yours.
